## Batch 3 priority guards

1. Do not place the main News attribution in paragraph 2 by default.
2. Avoid another NEW title beginning with a gerund or using a colon/subtitle unless genre-essential.
3. Reduce “not X but Y” and other binary contrast hinges.
4. Avoid object-led scene setting in analytical, Government and Proposal openings.
5. Do not use a polite direct question as the core Business approval request.
6. Give Legal texts a different clause architecture where the assigned brief permits it.
7. Keep some endings at the same human or procedural scale rather than zooming out to a general lesson.

## Batch 4 priority signals

1. Treat contrast, evidence–limitation–action, and audit endings as editorial signals rather than automatic failures.
2. When a locked brief requires a case or object opening, state its wider relevance promptly.
3. Keep News attribution tied to verification or competing experience instead of returning to one fixed paragraph.
4. Vary Legal cadence without weakening obligations or inventing jurisdiction-dependent rules.
5. Let Proposal exceptions demonstrate access, timing or recipient impact instead of repeating a control checklist.

## Batch 5 priority signals

1. Treat title, opening, progression and cadence as connected choices; surface variation alone is insufficient.
2. Do not let manifest-required contrasts or problem–response sequences reproduce the same paragraph positions and closing action.
3. Place News and Government quotations according to evidential function, not a rotation formula.
4. Keep Business ownership clear without making every final sentence a polite approval request.
5. Use Legal and Proposal exceptions to expose the specific authority, custody, consent, access or delivery consequence at stake.
6. Preserve natural genre language where conventional phrasing is necessary; no additional numerical rejection threshold is introduced.

The dedicated midpoint audit reviews all 70 approved texts. Its detailed evidence is recorded in `../midpoint-audit/english-corpus-v2-midpoint-audit.xlsx`. The register below is intentionally concise and replaces the Batch 5 drafting priorities for second-half planning.

| Formal-category lexical convergence | News ↔ Government has the highest category-level lexical similarity, followed by Government ↔ Proposals.
